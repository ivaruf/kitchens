- **Plan meals without choosing a kitchen first.** Asked for 2026-10-02.
  Dive into suggestions and a meal plan straight from the front door, across
  kitchens. Later there may be more cuisines and styles that mix. A week will
  usually stay mostly within one kitchen, but must not have to.
  Plus **plain sides the kids will eat**: plain fried rice, potatoes with
  ketchup, the "boring" things that sit beside an adventurous main.
  Being scoped one question at a time.
  - *Where plain sides live:* **their own kitchen**, an "everyday" kitchen of
    basics that every week plan draws from, and that can grow. The owner asked
    for suggestions of what goes in it.
  - *What it starts with:* **all four groups**: rice, potatoes, pasta and
    noodles (these for sure), and simple veg. It can grow into many simple
    dishes and sides. Simple veg stays even though only the older child (9)
    would eat it and the younger (5) maybe only carrots: *"we need to keep
    serving still"*. Veg is offered at every table, not left out because it
    may be refused.
  - *Who the planner knows:* **keep it simple**: no people, no "children"
    switch. Every planned meal gets a plain side and a veg from the everyday
    kitchen, whatever the main is.
  - *How the planner starts:* **pick a kitchen mood**: "Greek week",
    "Vietnamese week" or "mix it up", then it proposes the week.
  - *How many dinners:* **you choose**, 3 to 7, when you start.
  - *Is a plan kept:* **not yet** — nothing saved to the device for now. The
    owner is still finding the right layout; persistence comes after.
  - **Built rough, 2026-10-02**, to judge the layout: "Plan the week" door on
    the front page, moods, 3–7 dinners, swap any main/side/veg, one shopping
    list, copy. An everyday kitchen (js/everyday.js) holds the plain sides.
  - *Children's sides:* the children **can** eat egg, milk and gluten, so the
    everyday kitchen is written the **ordinary way** — buttered pasta, egg
    fried rice, wheat pasta, mash with milk and butter — with the free
    version as a note for the plate that needs it.
