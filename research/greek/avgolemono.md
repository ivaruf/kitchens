# Chicken avgolemono, kotosoupa avgolemono (Κοτόσουπα αυγολέμονο), consensus research

Researched 2026-10-04.

**What changed compared with the current recipe in `js/recipes.js`, and why.** The current recipe is close to the consensus. These are the changes:

- **Servings: "6" instead of "4 to 6", and amounts normalised per 6.** Every whole-bird source cooks one pot of one bird. Nearly all of them say 6, or 6–8.
- **The bird is about 1.7 kg, not "small, 1.5 kg".** The sources run 1.4–2 kg, with a median of about 1.7.
- **Water is stated: about 3 litres.** The median is about 2.7–3 L for one bird. The current recipe leaves it out.
- **Rice goes up from 150 g to 180 g (about a cup).** The weighted median is 170–200 g per 6. Most sources say "1 cup".
- **Olive oil in the broth is added as optional.** It is in 6 of 14 sources (39 % weighted), and in no source is it essential.
- **Tempering is 3–4 ladles, not 4–5.** That is the median. Sources run from a few spoonfuls to a cup.
- **The `why` on beating the eggs now names the other half of the hub.** Whipping the whites to a soft meringue first is not a fringe trick. The weighted vote between whole eggs and separated eggs is a dead tie (8 vs 8).
- **A gluten note is added to `table`.** Orzo, fides or other small pasta in place of rice is common (Argiro, Kochilas/Saveur, Dimitra, My Prep Table, Meni Valle).

These stay the same:

- 2 eggs and 2 lemons (both exactly the median).
- Cold-water start and skimming.
- About an hour of poaching.
- Rice cooked in the strained broth.
- Finishing off the heat or on the lowest heat, never boiling.
- The meat returned to the pot.

## Method

All counts are **weighted** (native hands-on = 2, ordinary = 1, aggregator = 0.5). Plain n/N is also given. Egg decisions count the 13 egg-lemon sources only.

- **Cold-water start.** 8 sources say so outright, and most others imply it (the chicken is "covered with water"). paxxi boils hard for 10 min before skimming. SM starts in hot broth.
- **Skim the foam.** 9 sources say so explicitly.
- **Lid while poaching.**
  - Ajar: Cookout, paxxi, MPT.
  - On: Argiro, CK, OCS.
  - **Consensus: ajar.**
- **Poaching time.** Cookout 50–60 · Argiro 75 · paxxi 55–60 · Nikolopaa 60 · Mary 60 · CK 75 · MPT 60–75 · live 60 · Dimitra 60–90 · OCS 120 min. **Median about 60–75 min.**
- **Strain, then cook the rice in the broth.** Nearly every source. The median is 15–20 min.
- **Whole eggs or separated (whites whipped to a meringue).**
  - Whole: 8/13 (weight 8). Cookout, Argiro, SM, CK, live, OCS, HS, Kochilas/Saveur.
  - Separated: 4/13 (weight 8). paxxi, Nikolopaa, Mary, Dimitra.
  - Yolks only: 1/13 (weight 1). iCtH.
  - **A weighted tie.** Whole eggs win on count, so they stay in the recipe, beaten well. The meringue way is named in the `why`.
- **Lemon into the eggs before the broth.** All 13.
- **Tempering amount.** Cookout 3–4 ladles · SM 5–6 spoonfuls · Nikolopaa 1 cup · Kochilas 1 cup · Mary 5–6 ladles · iCtH 5–6 spoonfuls · Argiro "a ladle at a time". **Median about 3–4 ladles.**
- **Heat after the egg-lemon goes in.**
  - Off the heat: 5/13 (7.5). paxxi, Nikolopaa, OCS, SM, Mary.
  - Lowest heat for 1–5 min without boiling: 6/13 (7.5). Cookout, Argiro, CK, HS, Dimitra, Kochilas.
  - live-kitchen brings it back to the boil, an outlier.
  - **Consensus: either way, never boiling.** That is what the current recipe already says.
- **Chicken back in the soup or served on the side.**
  - Back in: 10/14 (12).
  - Separately or into each bowl: 4/14 (7). Argiro, SM, Nikolopaa, paxxi.
  - **Consensus: back in, with "or on the side" as a serving note.**

**Variants**, noted and not used:

- **Orzo, fides or noodles** in place of rice (gluten).
- **Potato blended into the broth** (live, HS).
- **Saffron and wine** (Kochilas's TV version).
- **Cornflour as insurance** against curdling (Argiro).
- **Chicken as a second course**, with lemon and oil (several Greek households).

## Confidence

**Strong agreement:**

- One bird, cold start, skim, about an hour of poaching.
- Rice cooked in the strained broth.
- 2 eggs and 2 lemons for a 6-serving pot. Both medians are clean.
- Lemon into the eggs, then the broth.
- Never boil once the egg is in.

**Genuinely split:**

- **Whole versus separated eggs.** A weighted tie: the Greek family blogs lean towards whipped whites, the Greek chefs and editorial sites towards whole eggs.
- **Off the heat versus a minute on the lowest heat.** Also a tie, which the recipe covers by doing both: off the heat to pour, then the lowest heat to warm through.
- **Rice amount.** It varies with how thick a family likes it (80–300 g per 6). 180 g sits in the middle.

**Assumptions to note:**

- **Servings were assumed** for 5 sources (SM, Mary, CK, OCS, Saveur), which shifts the per-6 egg, lemon and rice figures a little.
- **My Prep Table makes no egg-lemon** and counts for the broth only.
- **Kochilas/Saveur was read from a search extract.**
