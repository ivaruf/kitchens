# Gigantes plaki — consensus research

Date researched: 2026-10-04

The recipe in `js/recipes.js` has the right shape: soak, pre-boil, make a sauce on the stove, bake uncovered at 180 °C. Nineteen sources agree on that. Five things change. **Dill is a minority herb.** Only 4 of 19 sources use it (weighted 25 %), so it drops out of `key` and becomes optional, and the `line` stops promising it. Parsley is the herb most cooks reach for (12/19), and dried oregano is close behind (10/19). **Carrot and celery double:** the median is 2 carrots and 2 stalks for 500 g of beans, not 1 of each. **Tomato paste goes to 2 tbsp.** **The beans go into the tin with a real ladle of their water (about 250 ml)**, not a splash. Nearly every source adds 120–700 ml of liquid at the bake, and that liquid is what turns the beans creamy. **The bake runs about 1 hour, up to 1¼ hours**, not 45–60 minutes. Oil comes down slightly, from 150 to 125 ml. The half-cup most Greek cooks use is about 120 ml.

## Method

All counts below are **weighted** (2 / 1 / 0.5, total 28.5), with the raw source count in brackets.

- **Soak overnight**: every source that starts from dried beans (17).
- **Pre-cook before baking**: every dried-bean source (17, weight 26.5). None bakes raw soaked beans, except My Greek Dish's alternative 2-hour covered method, which it gives second. Time: 40–90 min, median **50 min**. Wording: "until nearly tender, holding their shape, not soft" (BBC "al dente", My Greek Dish "not fully cooked", Kopiaste "almost cooked", Argiro "keeping their shape", Mia Kouppa "biteable but crumbly inside"). The beans finish in the oven.
- **Fresh water for boiling**: majority. Salting the boiling water is split (Dimitra, My Greek Dish, Konstantina and Kopiaste salt it; BBC says don't). Not decided either way.
- **Sauce cooked on the stove first**: 13 sources, weight ~19. Everything raw into the tin: Mia Kouppa and Konstantina, weight 4. Staged in the oven: My Greek Dish, weight 2.
- **Bean water / water with the beans in the tin**: 10 sources, weight ~15.5. Median ~300 ml. The rest rely on a thin sauce or tinned juice.
- **Oven temperature**: 165–200 °C, median **180 °C** (11 sources at 175–180).
- **Covered or not**: uncovered throughout, 10 sources (weight 14.5). Covered then uncovered, 4 (weight 7). Covered the whole time, Akis (2). Four don't say. → **uncovered**, with "cover for the first half if your beans went in firm" as the minority practice.
- **Bake time** (beans pre-boiled): 55–120 min, median **60 min**, upper quartile 90. The 25–30 min bakes (#17, #19) are gratins over fully cooked beans and are left out.
- **Done when**: the sauce has thickened down to its oil and the top and edges catch (Argiro "caramelise in the oil", Sia "until it caramelises", Kopiaste, BBC "browned and slightly crisp").
- **Regional/family variants**, noted and not followed: northern Greece (Kastoria, Prespes, Florina) adds roasted Florina peppers and sometimes smoked paprika or cumin (#1, #3, #15). Leek in place of some onion (#5). Honey and balsamic (Kochilas). Orange slices (Katerina). Cinnamon (BBC, Akis). Pork belly or sausage (LiFO, the Greek Foodie). Feta baked on top (Dimitra, Cantina). The garlic-and-dill *skordati* from the Cyclades (Kremezi) has no tomato at all.

## Confidence

The sources agree strongly on the method. All of them soak and pre-boil to nearly tender. Most cook the sauce on the stove first, bake at about 180 °C for about an hour, and leave the tin uncovered. They agree on onion, garlic, tomato and plenty of olive oil, and that parsley is the main herb. Dill is the clearest finding: the current recipe makes it a key ingredient, but only a quarter of cooks use it, mostly in the north or in "with vegetables" versions. They agree less on tomato quantity. The unweighted median is 700 g and the weighted median 500 g, because the Greek home cooks run lighter on tomato than the English sites do; 600 g sits between them. Oil spans 56–240 ml; the half cup is the Greek default, but the sources are evenly spread around it. Oregano (49 %) and sugar (49 %) are on the line between "most cooks" and "optional". Oregano is kept as a listed ingredient and sugar only as a correction. Vefa Alexiadou could not be checked, and the Akis amounts come from a repost, because his site blocks fetching.
