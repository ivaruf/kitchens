# Fakes (Φακές), consensus research

Researched 2026-10-04.

**What changed compared with the current recipe in `js/recipes.js`, and why.** The shape of the current recipe is right: no soak, soften the base, simmer, vinegar at the end. The quantities and two of the ingredients move.

- **Tomato means real tomato now, not just paste.** 12 of 14 sources (weighted 20.5 of 23.5) use grated, canned, passata or juiced tomato, at about 250 ml for 4. Paste appears in only 6 of 14 (47 % weighted), nearly always beside it. Paste therefore becomes an optional extra and tomato joins `key`.
- **More lentils: 300 g for 4, up from 250 g.** That is the weighted median, with a kept range of 167–500 g.
- **Carrot is standard, no longer optional.** 11 of 14 sources (17.5 of 23.5) use it.
- **Celery is added as optional.** It is in 6 of 14 (36 % weighted).
- **Oregano is now marked optional.** It is in 4 of 14 (30 %).
- **The lid goes on.** Every source that says anything covers the pot (9).
- **The simmer is 40–45 min,** the median of 13 sources.
- **Vinegar goes up to 1–2 tbsp in the pot, and more at the table.** All 14 sources use vinegar. 7 add it to the pot at the end and 4 only at the table.
- **More oil at the table.** 8 sources finish with extra oil. The amount in the pot stays 80 ml, which is exactly the weighted median.
- **The `why` on rinsing now mentions the optional blanch.** Bringing the lentils to the boil and draining them, "the first water", is done by 6 of 14 (weighted 9.5 vs 14). That is common, but it is not the majority.

## Method

All counts are **weighted** (native hands-on = 2, ordinary = 1, aggregator = 0.5) out of 23.5. Plain n/N is also given.

- **Soak the lentils.**
  - No: 12/14.
  - A short soak instead: Mamazilla 10 min, SBS 1 h, MiaK 3–4 h as an option.
  - **Consensus: no soak.**
- **Blanch the lentils (bring to the boil in fresh water, drain, throw the water away).**
  - Yes: 6/14 (9.5). MGD, Dimitra, Cookout, GCT, Argiro, Xrys.
  - No: 8/14 (14).
  - **Consensus: rinse only.** The blanch is mentioned as an option.
- **Soften the base in oil first.**
  - Yes: 10/14 (17).
  - No, everything goes in together: 4/14 (6.5).
  - The median softening time is about 8 min (range 3–10). Garlic and paste go in at the end of it.
- **All the oil at the start or split.** Most put it all in at the start (Kochilas, Dimitra, MiaK, Mamazilla, SftS, Eleni, Cantina, GCbK). Cookout and Argiro hold half back for the end. 8/14 add **extra oil at the table**. **Consensus: all in at the start, more at the table.**
- **Lid.** On in 9/9 of the sources that say.
- **Simmer time.** MGD 40 · Kochilas 60 · Dimitra 30 · MiaK 30 · Cookout 75 · GCbK 40 · Mamazilla 40 · SftS 45 · Eleni 50 · SBS 45 · Cantina 45 · GCT 65 · Argiro 35 min. **Median 45 min.**
- **Salt.** During cooking, usually with the water or midway, in most sources. GCbK and Dina Nikolaou (seen in a search extract) salt in the last 10 min.
- **Vinegar timing.**
  - At the end, in the pot: 7/14 (13).
  - At the table only: 4/14 (7).
  - With the water at the start: 2/14 (3).
  - **Consensus: off the heat at the end, with more at the table.**

**Variants**, noted and not used:

- **Balsamic vinegar** (Cantina).
- **Petimezi or grape molasses** for sweetness (Kochilas).
- **A handful of rice** for a heartier pot (Dimitra, MiaK).
- **Florina pepper and cumin** (Cookout).
- **Leek** (Argiro).

## Confidence

**Very strong agreement:**

- Onion, garlic, bay and vinegar are in all 14 sources.
- Tomato in some fresh or canned form: 12 of 14.
- No soak, sauté first (10 of 14), lid on.
- About 45 min of simmering.
- The lentil and oil medians are robust: 300 g and 80 ml for 4.

**Real split:**

- **Blanching the lentils and throwing that water away.** 6 of 14 do, almost all of them Greek-language or Greek-family cooks. Weighted, it still loses 9.5 to 14, so it stays an optional note.

**Assumptions to note:**

- **Servings were assumed** for Dimitra's (6) and Xrysoskoufaki (4).
- **Argiro's recipe was read from a truncated extract.** Her amounts are complete; her last step is not.
