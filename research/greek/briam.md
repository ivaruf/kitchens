# Briam (Μπριάμ) — consensus research

Researched 2026-10-04.

**What changed vs `js/recipes.js`, and why.** The current recipe has the right
shape: vegetables tossed together, not layered; no water; 190 °C; tomato,
parsley and oregano. Where it parts from the sources is in the amounts and one
step of the method. Most sources **cover the tin for the first ~45 minutes**,
then uncover it to brown. That holds for 10 of 16 recipes, and for 15.5 of
24.5 once the counts are weighted. The current recipe roasts uncovered from
the start. The sources also use **less of almost everything** for four people:
the median is 2 courgettes, not 3, and 1 onion, not 2. Oil is 100 ml, not
150 ml (the weighted median was 90 ml). Garlic is 3 cloves, not 4. Tomato is
about 350 g, not 4 whole tomatoes. Peppers go to 1–2, potatoes are given as
about 500 g, and the aubergine is pinned at about 400 g. **Oregano doubles to
2 tsp.** One more step is written in: spread the vegetables in one loose
layer, not piled up, and stir them gently once when the foil comes off.

## Method

All counts below are **weighted** as described under Sources (out of 24.5),
with raw counts in brackets.

- **Tossed, not layered.** Tossed: 17.5 (11). Layered in rows: 7 (5: sources
  7, 11, 12, 13, 14). The layered rows are a real style, but a minority one.
- **Covered first, then uncovered.** Covered: 15.5 (10). Uncovered
  throughout: 9 (6: sources 2, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11). Kochilas keeps the cover on
  the whole time.
- **Time covered:** median 45 min (range 40–60). **Time uncovered:** median
  about 40 min (range 30–60). Recipes that roast uncovered throughout take
  60–80 min. Total: about 1½ hours in the oven.
- **Oven temperature.** 200 °C: 11.5 (8, counting Akis's 180 °C fan as
  200 °C conventional). 190 °C: 6 (3). 175–180 °C: 7 (5). Weighted median
  190 °C; most sources sit between 190 and 200 °C.
- **Water.** None: 14 (9). Water added: 10.5 (7, 80–185 ml per 4). With the
  tin covered, the courgettes and tomato give enough liquid. Several sources
  that add no water still say to add a splash if the tin dries before the
  potatoes are soft (5, 9).
- **Stir.** Gently, once, when the cover comes off (4, 9, 13). Nobody turns
  it repeatedly.
- **Spread out, not piled up** (4, 5). Charred edges are the stated goal in
  4, 12 and 14.
- **Pre-cooking.** None: 22.5 (14). Pre-frying the aubergine and onion (10),
  or salting the aubergine (11), is a minority step.
- **Cut.** Slices or rounds (9) vs chunks (7): split. Potatoes thinner or
  smaller than the rest (5, 16, and implied by most).
- **Serve.** Warm or at room temperature; rested 15–20 minutes (4, 9); better
  the next day (6). With feta and bread, everywhere.

Variants, noted but not driving the consensus:
- Mint (Argiro, Olive Tomato) or dill (Kochilas).
- Carrots (Syrigos, Akis).
- A spoon of honey or sugar against sharp tomatoes (Akis, Dimitra).
- A splash of wine (Crete).
- Layered rows, Kochilas style, with many green peppers and no aubergine.
- The name *tourlou* for the same dish.

## Confidence

**Strong agreement** on the core: potato, courgette, onion, tomato, olive oil
and parsley are in nearly every source. Tossed beats layered. Nothing is
pre-cooked. The tin is baked until the liquid has gone and the edges char,
and served at room temperature with feta and bread.

**Fair agreement:**
- Covering for the first half (about 2:1 weighted).
- An oven at 190–200 °C.
- Aubergine and pepper are usual, at 3 of 4 sources, but not universal.

**Weak:**
- Water is a near split, and "none, a splash if dry" wins narrowly.
- Oil varies about threefold (53–160 ml per 4). The 100 ml given rounds up
  from a 90 ml weighted median.
- Absolute vegetable amounts scatter widely, because "a potato" and "an
  aubergine" vary so much in size. The grams are the median, not a precise
  figure.

**Not checked:**
- Akis's baking time; his own site returned 403.
- Servings for iefimerida, Cretan Gastronomy and Cantina, which were assumed.
